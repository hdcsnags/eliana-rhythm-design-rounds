How to look

  1. Open each one on your phone and use it for a minute: the menu at the bottom, the Bible, the buttons on the first screen.
  2. Each design changes with the time of day. Tap Morning, Midday, Evening or Night near the top of the first screen to see the others.
  3. Use your browser's back button to return here.
  4. Tell the person who sent you this link which letter you would want to use every day, and one thing you liked or disliked about each.

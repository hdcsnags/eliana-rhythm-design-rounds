How to look

  1. Each page shows the phone screens side by side, labelled by letter. Scroll sideways to see them all; a computer or tablet is easier than a phone.
  2. Each screen scrolls and most buttons work, so tap around inside it.
  3. Under each one you can mark it and type what you like or dislike.
  4. Press Copy my picks at the top and send the copied text back to the person who gave you this link.

The letters are not the same designs from one round to the next. Your marks stay on your own device.

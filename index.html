Eliana's Rhythm: design ideas

These are mockups, not the app. Seven AI models were each asked how Eliana's Rhythm could look and work, without seeing each other's answers. The words inside them are placeholders.

Round 2The newer set. The models were told everything the app has. Round 1The first set, from a shorter description.

How to look

  1. Each page shows seven phone screens side by side, labelled A to G. Scroll sideways to see them all; a computer or tablet is easier than a phone.
  2. Each screen scrolls and most buttons work, so tap around inside it.
  3. Under each one you can mark it and type what you like or dislike.
  4. Press Copy my picks at the top and send the copied text back to the person who gave you this link.

The letters are not the same designs in both rounds. Your marks stay on your own device.

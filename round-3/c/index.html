har·pū ū·ḏə·‘ū kî ’ā·nō·ḵî ’ĕ·lō·hîm

✦ Fresh rendering

Let your hands fall. Then you will know that I am God.

Deeper lens

The first word is a Hiphil imperative plural of rāp̄āh, to slacken or let drop. It is spoken to a crowd, and the psalm around it is full of war. It reads less like an invitation to calm and more like an order to put the weapons down.

Everyday insight

Stillness here is not the absence of noise. It is the moment you stop holding the thing up yourself and find that it does not fall.

Take-home practice

Name one thing you are gripping. Open your hand, literally, and say the verse once.

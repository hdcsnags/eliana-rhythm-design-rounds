“That light keeps shining right inside the dark — and the dark could neither snuff it out nor grasp its secret.”

Carry It With You

Light does not wait for the room to cooperate before it begins to shine. Bring one honest corner of your morning into His presence.

Notice This

John shifts from past tense (“was” in vv. 1–4) into present tense here: phainei — the light is actively shining right now.

Why It Matters

Darkness does not need to disappear before hope is real. Even small fidelity outlasts heavy nights.

Why does John say the darkness has not “overcome” the light?
✦ Eliana
The Greek verb katelaben (Strong's G2638) carries a deliberate double edge: it means both overpower / extinguish and comprehend / grasp. Even when darkness does not understand the light, it cannot put it out.
